Most days, we move through the world without really noticing it.
But every so often, something ordinary asks us to slow down and pay attention.
A familiar voice. The light across a kitchen table.
The quiet rhythm of a place we know by heart.
These moments do not ask us to do more.
They simply ask us to be here, one small detail at a time.
Listening is a way of making room for the world.
And perhaps that is where understanding begins.
