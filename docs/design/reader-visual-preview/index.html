The art of paying attention

已标记 2

Most days, we move through the world
without really noticing it.

大多数日子里，我们穿行于世界，却并未真正留意它。

But every so often, something
asks us to slow down and pay attention.

但偶尔，一件平常的小事，会让我们慢下来，认真留意。

A familiar voice. The light across a kitchen table.
The quiet of a place we know by heart.

一个熟悉的声音，洒在厨房桌上的光，还有熟悉之地静静的节奏。

These moments do not ask us to do more.
They simply ask us to be here.

这些瞬间不要求我们多做什么，只希望我们在场。

Listening is a way of making room for the world,
one small detail at a time.

倾听，是为世界留出空间，一次留意一个小小的细节。

And perhaps that is where understanding begins:
with the simple decision to notice.

也许理解正是这样开始的：从决定认真留意的那一刻起。

读到这里，也是一次积累。
